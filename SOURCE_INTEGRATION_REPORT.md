## Media and copyright decisions

The revision uses the following rule: **copyright status is not changed merely by cropping, recoloring, tracing, restyling, or asking a generative system to imitate an existing protected image**. A superficial modification was therefore never treated as a copyright cure.

- Original diagrams were rebuilt as native SVGs from concepts or primary numerical results.
- Videos and film/television examples are linked and framed with a prediction, observation question, mechanism, and boundary condition; their frames are not reproduced.
- Journal, book, news, advertisement, website, and commercial-deck screenshots were not copied unless rights were independently clear.
- Book-extracted charts and tables in DPN07 were replaced with prose, original tools, or primary-study descriptions rather than copied or cosmetically altered.
- Stock, AI-generated, and unexplained section art in the decks was not reused because provenance and publication rights were not established.
- Unused inherited image parts inside later DOCX packages were treated as residue, not as source illustrations.
- All retained figures include meaningful alternative text and are checked for text collision, spacing, connector attachment, and arrow direction.

Appendix B gathers the linked teaching videos. Availability and rights remain with the linked providers.

## Terminology: “transfer” and “worked transfer”

In the source manuscripts, **transfer** means carrying a mechanism, diagnostic question, or tool into a different domain—for example, applying opportunity cost to a negotiation or applying attention design to a meeting. **Worked transfer** means that the text models the move step by step rather than merely asserting that the idea also applies elsewhere.

Because these labels can sound technical or ambiguous to readers, visible section headings in the revised book normally use **Apply the model** or **Worked application**. The underlying teaching practice remains: diagnose a concrete case, name the mechanism, test its boundary, and show what action changes.

## Verification still required at publication time

External links can disappear, and scientific conclusions can change. Before formal publication, the author should perform a final link check, verify permissions for any subsequently added third-party media, and subject the full bibliography to normal copyediting. The current build treats linked media as illustration rather than evidence and states major scientific boundary conditions in the chapter where the claim appears.
