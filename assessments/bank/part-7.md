## C42.05 | action-threshold | hard | apply | 42

+ p > 0.2, with indifference at p = 0.2 | Compare the certain cost 2 with the expected unprotected loss 10p.
- p > 0.5, with indifference at p = 0.5 | A majority-probability rule ignores the unequal consequences.
- p > 0.8, with indifference at p = 0.8 | This uses the complement of the actual threshold.
- Any p greater than zero | A small rain chance may not justify the certain inconvenience.
- Only p = 1 | Certainty is unnecessary when the expected avoided loss exceeds the cost.
