## C30.01 | attitude-and-action | easy | apply | 30
Employees agree that a new reporting tool is useful but do not use it because no training time is available. What does the case most clearly show?
+ Favorable attitudes need not produce action when implementation is blocked. | Agreement and feasible follow-through are distinct outcomes.
- Lack of use proves the employees privately oppose the tool. | The stated barrier is training time.
- More favorable attitudes remove the need for training. | Belief change does not supply practical capability.
- Public agreement is a direct measure of technical competence. | Agreement does not measure ability to use the tool.
- Every implementation problem is a credibility problem. | The obstacle here concerns resources rather than source credibility.

## C32.03 | claim-evidence-match | medium | apply | 32
