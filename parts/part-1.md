# Part 1. The Architecture of Choice

*From the visible choice to the hidden processes of attention, prediction, valuation, and expectation.*

<div class="part-overview"><strong>Why this part comes here</strong><p>The book begins upstream of choice. Students first need a functional map of a decision, a benchmark for coherence, and a psychologically realistic account of what becomes evidence and value.</p></div>

## Guiding questions
