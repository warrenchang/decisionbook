# Chapter source filenames

The 41 canonical `.qmd` files are numbered in the book's current reading order
and use a lowercase form of the current chapter title. For example, Chapter 8,
*Fast and Frugal Thinking*, is `08-fast-and-frugal-thinking.qmd`.

Files beginning with `retired-` preserve material from chapters merged during
the structural revision. They are not rendered as separate chapters. The
remaining `.md` files belong to the earlier hand-built edition and are also not
used by the current Quarto book.
