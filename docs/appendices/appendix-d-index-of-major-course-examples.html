Appendix D — Index of Major Examples

Use this index to revisit the cases, demonstrations, and worked examples most likely to appear in class discussion and application questions. Evidence role states what the example can do. A demonstration or illustration can reveal a possibility; it is not by itself evidence of prevalence, effect size, causality, or generality.
