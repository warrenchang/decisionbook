Part 5. Persuasion, Story, and Connection

How messages update models, stories create simulated meaning, and conversation builds shared understanding.

Why this part comes here

Persuasion, storytelling, and communication are related but not identical. The sequence moves from intentional model change, to narrative simulation, to the joint construction and repair of meaning.
