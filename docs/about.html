About This Book

This book grew from courses in Behavioral Economics and decision, persuasion, and negotiation taught and refined over several years. It brings together decision science, persuasion, negotiation, communication, and behavior design as one cumulative journey: from the hidden process beneath a choice, through judgment under uncertainty and interaction with other minds, to the design of environments and institutions that can learn.

The book’s recurring ethical test is whether a message, environment, conversation, agreement, or decision system would remain defensible if the people affected understood how it worked, whose objective it served, what evidence supported it, and how they could refuse, correct, or revise it.

About the Author

Huanren Warren Zhang is Associate Professor at Aalborg University Business School and the University of Southern Denmark.

He is a behavioral economist who uses experiments, game theory, computational models, and empirical analysis to understand human and organizational behavior. His research examines strategic and risky decision-making, social norms, social preferences, social dilemmas, coordination, and human and organizational learning.
