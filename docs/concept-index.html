Index of Concepts

Online search finds every occurrence of a word. This index does something narrower: it points to the chapter where a concept receives its main explanation and to selected places where the book extends, tests, or applies it. It is a map of the cumulative argument, not a concordance. Major cases and demonstrations are indexed separately in Appendix B.
