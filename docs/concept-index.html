Index of Concepts

Search finds every occurrence of a word. This index instead points to the place where a concept receives its fullest explanation and, selectively, to places where the book extends, tests, or applies it. Main treatment identifies the primary explanation; related links identify substantive extensions rather than incidental mentions. See also connects ideas that are easy to confuse or especially useful together. Major demonstrations and cases remain indexed separately in Appendix B.
