Part 1. The Architecture of Choice

From the visible choice to the hidden processes of attention, prediction, valuation, and expectation.

Why this part comes here

The book begins upstream of choice. Students first need a functional map of a decision, a benchmark for coherence, and a psychologically realistic account of what becomes evidence and value.

Guiding questions

What happens before a visible choice?
What would coherent choice require?
How do attention, prediction, value, and expectation shape the decision?
