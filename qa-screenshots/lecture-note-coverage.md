## Image-rights rule used in the revision

- Original conceptual diagrams were rebuilt in the book's own SVG design language.
- Empirical findings and data patterns are paraphrased or redrawn only with source attribution; slide styling is not copied.
- Third-party photos, film and video frames, product packaging, logos, advertisements, screenshots, and book covers were not imported.
- Public-domain material may be described or quoted within ordinary scholarly limits, but images were still omitted unless provenance was clear.
- A superficial crop, recolor, filter, or AI restyle was not treated as a copyright cure. When provenance was uncertain, the visual was replaced by an original explanatory diagram or by attributed prose.

This is a conservative editorial rights screen, not a legal opinion.
