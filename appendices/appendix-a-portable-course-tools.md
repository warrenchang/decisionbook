# Appendix A. Portable Course Tools

<aside class="callout one-page-decision-journal"><div class="callout-title">ONE-PAGE DECISION JOURNAL</div><div class="callout-body">Decision and deadline; alternatives considered; best feasible alternative forgone; predictions with probabilities; values and trade-offs; assumptions; emotional and social context; review date. After the outcome: compare forecast with result, separate process from luck, and state what should and should not change.</div></aside>

<aside class="callout attention-audit"><div class="callout-title">ATTENTION AUDIT</div><div class="callout-body">What captured attention? What was missing? Which question, role, metric, order, device, or time pressure made that pattern likely? What design would make the neglected variable visible?</div></aside>

<aside class="callout behavior-redesign-canvas"><div class="callout-title">BEHAVIOR REDESIGN CANVAS</div><div class="callout-body">Target behavior; cue ecology; urge or local problem; current response; immediate reward or relief; smallest replacement; friction change; prompt; immediate reinforcement; measurement; recovery plan; ethical check.</div></aside>

<aside class="callout persuasion-canvas"><div class="callout-title">PERSUASION CANVAS</div><div class="callout-body">Audience current model; protected identity; barrier to change; meaningful mismatch; new causal model; credible evidence; appropriate emotion; source credibility; agency-preserving action; uncertainty and counterargument.</div></aside>

<aside class="callout layered-listening"><div class="callout-title">LAYERED LISTENING</div><div class="callout-body">WHAT happened? HOW did it feel or unfold? WHY does it matter? WHO is implicated in identity or relationship? WHEN is the timing or history relevant? ACTION: what does the person want now - understanding, advice, decision, repair, or commitment?</div></aside>

<aside class="callout negotiation-preparation"><div class="callout-title">NEGOTIATION PREPARATION</div><div class="callout-body">BATNA and how to improve it; reservation value; target; opening offer; objective standards; issues and priorities; estimate of their interests and alternatives; questions; packages; concession plan; ethical boundaries; implementation and review.</div></aside>
