## Imagined surroundings prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Create a clean flat editorial illustration triptych for a behavioral economics textbook. Ultra-wide 3:1 canvas, three equally sized nearly square panels side by side, with white gutters. Each panel shows a DIFFERENT possible imagined environment at the same broad neighborhood scale. LEFT: dense gray apartment buildings across a city block, buildings fill the whole background, clean paved road, no park or lawn. CENTER: a broad green landscaped park stretching into the distance with a path, small benches and many sage-green trees, no buildings visible. RIGHT: an urban gray street with widespread shallow blue floodwater covering the road and sidewalks beside apartment buildings; NO rain, NO clouds of rain, NO hydrant visible, NO large foreground object. Colors/style: restrained pale navy and blue-gray buildings, sage-green foliage, pale blue skies and water, crisp editorial shapes with gentle shading, no photographic detail. Every panel uses a wide view with buildings or trees at the scale of a whole neighborhood, not closeups. No frames, borders around objects, people, words, letters, arrows, labels, captions or watermarks. These are illustrative imagined surroundings; don't connect the panels as one scene. Keep the foreground center of each panel simple because a separate window showing observed information will be placed there.
