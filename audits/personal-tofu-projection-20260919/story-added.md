## From my experience: does tofu need to replace cheese?

Even after moving to Denmark, it still often does not occur to me to include cheese in a meal.

This brings back an exchange from my time studying in the United States. An English teacher on a training course for international graduate teaching assistants once asked me whether tofu was a substitute for cheese in China. I did not know what to say. I knew little about how Americans ate cheese, and I had never thought of tofu as filling a place that cheese would otherwise occupy in my meals.

Looking back, I could have explained that there was no gap to fill: the meals I grew up with did not need a substitute for cheese. Her question seemed to start with a familiar food and look for its counterpart in another cuisine. The exchange reminds me how easily our own experience can define the question we ask about someone else’s life. Asking “How do you use tofu in your meals?” would have given me a clearer starting point for explaining mine.
