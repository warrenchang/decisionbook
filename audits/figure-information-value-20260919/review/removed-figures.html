Figure 25.2 · original placement

Ultimatum, dictator, and menu variants

Original caption

The comparison covers the ultimatum, dictator, and take-or-menu-variant games. Removing the responder's rejection option separates strategic rejection from giving; changing the allocator's ability to give, take, or leave the study changes property rights and the meaning of the observed allocation. Why I removed it

Reason to retain or redesign

Actual game trees, allocations, or action menus could make the treatment differences intuitive.
