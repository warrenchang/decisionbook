# Chapter 6: music and cultural reward

## Editorial scope

Integrated the three requested studies in “Music and cultural objects can be rewarding,” after the discussion of social value. The narrative distinguishes individual willingness to pay, attractiveness, and aggregate sales. Full verified references were added to the chapter; Salimpoor's title uses “predict,” not “predicts.” This is a focused teaching addition, not a systematic review.

## Synthesis

Salimpoor connects sensory processing to individual willingness to pay; Erk extends the examples to manufactured goods; Berns and Moore changes the target to aggregate market outcomes. These are complementary questions rather than direct replications. No claim is made that the three studies identify one reward mechanism or measure normative value. The invitation example remains the chapter's organizing application.

General claims about causal learning, cross-cultural universality, or dependable commercial forecasting would require additional evidence. Those claims are not needed for this addition and are not made.
