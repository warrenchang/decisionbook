## Scientific and editorial boundaries

This was a complete reading and editorial revision of the owned chapters, not a new independent verification of every cited publication. No new empirical result or bibliography entry was introduced. The substantive clarifications concern definitions, causal interpretation of illustrative designs, and the provenance of an expanded teaching package. Examples and repeated instructions were condensed or developed where needed; the number of edits or a word-count reduction was not used as the target.

The root editor is responsible for the combined HTML/EPUB render, release QA, bibliography integration, cross-book checks, and any later user-authorized publication. The source contains some historical numeric table IDs; these were preserved to avoid breaking existing references during this focused editorial pass.
