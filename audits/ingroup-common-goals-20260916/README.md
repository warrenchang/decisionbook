# Ingroup favoritism, common goals, and cooperation

## Editorial decision

Chapter 25 is the main home for this discussion. The new section follows shared obligations and asks whom those obligations include. It then connects group boundaries to the chapter's concluding design discussion. The former short identity section in optional notes has been incorporated here, preserving its anchor and its Tajfel (1970) and Chen and Li (2009) references.

The sequence is: ingroup favoritism; superordinate goals; incentives and recategorization as distinct routes; common ingroup identity; dual identity; practical rebuilding and its limits. The joint-report example connects the section to the chapter's opening. Learning goals, the comparison table, Practice Lab, and closing synthesis now include group boundaries. Chapter 29 has one cross-reference. Three concept-index entries and five references were added.
