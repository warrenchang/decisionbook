## Deferred candidates

**Chapter 2, “Construct the feasible set.”** The imaginable set could contain overlapping feasible and considered regions, revealing both overlooked feasible options and considered-but-unavailable options. A nested three-set diagram would contradict the current text. However, the relation is already stated directly. Git commit `c43d562e713222668afee9b6f88e2f94e3ce8070` deliberately removed `option-information-portrait.svg` from the reading path on 19 September, retaining `fig-option-information` as an anchor and replacing the four-step framework with prose. The proposed overlap diagram would differ from that retired figure, but the current revision should still favor restraint.

**Chapter 13, context-dependent memory.** A 2×2 matrix could cross learning environment (land/underwater) with recall environment (land/underwater), making the matching-context comparison visible. The chapter already cites Godden and Baddeley (1975), Smith and Vela (2001), and Murre (2021). Its footnote reports that the later indoor-pool replication did not find the original advantage. If revisited, either label the graphic as an illustration of the experimental design or verify all four means and uncertainty from the primary study; keep the replication limitation visible in the caption. No new plot is justified in this pass.

**Lower-priority comparison screened: Hsee in Chapter 11.** The joint-versus-separate evaluation design is potentially visual, but the chapter explicitly states that the separate-evaluation contrast alone was not conventionally significant, despite the significant difference between evaluation modes. A schematic crossed-line reversal without verified means and uncertainty could overstate the finding. The chapter already has two substantial figures, and the present prose explains the reversal and its boundary clearly. This was a priority decision, not a conclusion that the study cannot be illustrated responsibly.

## Verification boundary

The review inspected source relationships and retained existing scientific qualifications. It did not re-estimate effects, retrieve primary study data, or inspect every existing figure at desktop and phone sizes. Destination visual QA belongs to the figures selected for revision elsewhere in this task.
