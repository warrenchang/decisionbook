## Placement decision

The material is split by explanatory job rather than placed in one long chapter.

| Location | Job | Reason |

| Chapter 22 | Prevent fitness, pleasure, meaning, autonomy, and moral worth from being collapsed | The well-being chapter is the natural place to state that evolutionary success is not the criterion for a good life. |

## Central claim boundary

Evolution can help explain why some cues and outcomes acquire motivational force. It does not prescribe what a person ought to value, imply that people consciously maximize reproductive success, or show that current preferences maximize either fitness or well-being. Any normative conclusion requires a separate argument about welfare, agency, rights, duties, relationships, and effects on others.

## Editorial checks

- Maslow's original proposal, the later pyramid graphic, and Kenrick et al.'s renovation are treated as three distinct objects.
- Developmental order, momentary motivational priority, and hypothesized evolutionary function are not conflated.
- Coherence, epistemic, instrumental, procedural, ecological, and resource standards of rationality are defined separately from evolutionary explanation.
- “Optimally irrational” is presented as a conditional question requiring an objective, constraints, environment, comparator, and predictive evidence.
- Adaptation, by-product, exaptation, learning, culture, mismatch, pathology, and current incentives remain live alternatives.
