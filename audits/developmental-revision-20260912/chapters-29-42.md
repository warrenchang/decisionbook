## Primary sources for substantive additions and corrections

- **Small, Loewenstein, and Slovic (2007), Study 3, pp. 148–149:** [authors' PDF](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/gl20/GeorgeLoewenstein/Papers_files/pdf/SympathyCallousness.pdf). 159 campus-recruited participants; five-dollar survey payment; mean donations 2.38 identifiable-only, 1.14 statistics-only, 1.43 combined. Combined was lower than identifiable-only; no claim that the combined-vs-statistical difference was significant. The paper does not validate separating/sequencing as a cure.
- **Kumkale and Albarracín (2004):** [author-lab PDF](https://socialactionlab.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Kumkale_The-Sleeper-Effect-in-Persuasion-A-Meta-Analytic-Review_2004.pdf), [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14717653/). Conditional delayed influence after discounting a communication, not a generic source-familiarity claim.
- **Berger and Heath (2007):** [original article DOI](https://doi.org/10.1086/519142), [author-posted manuscript](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/SSRN_ID1017174_code502159.pdf?abstractid=1017174&mirid=5). Identity-symbolic domains and divergence from others, rather than a study of content sharing.
- **Eyal and Epley (2010):** [primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20483849/). Matching construal levels to judge how one is seen.
- **Eyal, Steffel, and Epley (2018):** [article DOI](https://doi.org/10.1037/pspa0000115), [author institution record](https://cris.bgu.ac.il/en/publications/perspective-mistaking-accurately-understanding-the-mind-of-anothe-2/). 25 experiments; instructions to take perspective did not improve accuracy overall; direct conversation in the final experiment supplied useful information.
- **Kruger et al. (2005), p. 928, Study 2:** [original article PDF](https://uploads-ssl.webflow.com/5c484e0f4aa6f839dc553c45/5c86ca1f620e2b5879502a41_KrugerEpleyParkerNg2005.pdf). 29 analytic dyads after an excluded outlier dyad. Sender forecasts/actual performance in Figure 1 are reported approximately, not to unsupported tenths. Receiver self-assessment follows decoding and is reported as 89% email and 91% voice in the text.
- **Kumar and Epley (2023):** [primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35980709/). Kindness givers underestimate recipients' positive responses; no new numerical effect added.
- **Rackham original-author report:** [permission-marked reprint](https://www.scribd.com/document/368191672/The-Behavior-of-Successful-Negotiators). The report names the selection criteria, 48 negotiators/102 sessions, 38% versus 11% planning comments about common ground, and 21.3% versus 9.6% questions among coded negotiation behavior. Source is marked copyright 1980 Huthwaite; the new reference explicitly describes the consulted reprint. The original 1978 journal reference was retained rather than silently replaced.
- **Van Kleef, De Dreu, and Manstead (2004):** [primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14717628/). Computer-mediated emotional information influenced concessions via inferences about the opponent's limit; not evidence for every previously listed real-world moderator.
- **Loschelder et al. (2016):** [publisher abstract](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/0956797616666074/). Excessive precision can backfire with experts; a rationale can alter that response.
- **Gollwitzer (1999):** [author-lab record](https://www.socmot.uni-konstanz.de/publications/implementation-intentions-strong-effects-simple-plans), [original article PDF](https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/wp.nyu.edu/dist/c/6235/files/2019/02/gollwitzer-1999-implementation-intentions.pdf). Cue-linked implementation intentions; the reference already existed in the chapter.
- **Parry (2024):** [publisher article](https://doi.org/10.1080/15213269.2023.2286647). Limited, heterogeneous support for a smaller mere-presence effect; working-memory result distinguished from other outcomes and from an actual notification.
- **Iyengar and Lepper (2000), Study 1, pp. 996–997:** [original article PDF](https://faculty.washington.edu/jdb/345/345%20Articles/Iyengar%20%26%20Lepper%20%282000%29.pdf). 6/24 displays rotated hourly; approximately 30%/3% purchases among booth visitors. Purchase shelf contained the full selection. Stopped-at-booth denominators are not interchangeable with all passers-by.
- **Scheibehenne, Greifeneder, and Todd (2010):** [author PDF](https://www.scheibehenne.de/ScheibehenneGreifenederTodd2010.pdf). 50 experiments, virtually zero mean and substantial heterogeneity. **Chernev et al. (2015):** [publisher article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcps.2014.08.002). Choice complexity, task difficulty, preference uncertainty, and decision goals as moderators.
- **Dallacker et al. (2024):** [article DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.puhe.2024.08.009), [primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39305662/). Longitudinal five-country comparison; no average increase in deceased donation after the policy switch.
- **Haynes et al. (2009):** [primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19144931/). Eight hospitals; complications 11.0% to 7.0%, mortality 1.5% to 0.8%, before/after implementation. **Urbach et al. (2014):** [primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24620866/). 101 Ontario hospitals; no statistically significant reduction in complications or mortality after adoption.
- **Brynjolfsson, Li, and Raymond (2025):** [final QJE article](https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjae044). Abstract and Table 1 confirm 5,172 agents, so the existing number stands.
