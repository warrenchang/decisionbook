# Chapter and appendix revision plan

The seven parts can follow a preference as it becomes a consequential choice. The chapters then ask what changes when the people involved can examine how that choice formed. Keep the 42 chapter locations. Assign the full explanation of each mechanism to one chapter and use later appearances to answer a new question. The revised part introductions can name those questions and point to the cases that carry them forward.
