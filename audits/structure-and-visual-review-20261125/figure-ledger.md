**Keep** means the graphic already adds a visual comparison, demonstration or other useful relationship. **Use text or table** replaces the graphic while preserving its substantive content. **Consolidate** removes a duplicate treatment in favor of one shared explanation, display or case. **Redesign** retains a visual and changes what the reader can do with it. **Move** places it earlier, in another chapter, or in an optional section.

The visual screen records the pixel dimensions of the image inspected. All assets rendered, and the static images have matching source, HTML and EPUB bytes. Sheet sizes are listed for the complete survey. Where listed, the second size is a separate larger inspection. Figure 4.6 was screened as frames of the HTML animation. The book’s EPUB gives that activity an external demonstration link and a text description.
