## Evidence checked

- **Fogg's scope:** https://www.behaviormodel.org/ describes motivation, ability, and a prompt converging at the behavioral moment. B=MAP is presented as a diagnostic framework, not a quantitative multiplication law or a model restricted to habits. Donation and retail examples are original applications. Tiny Habits is distinguished as a practical method for arranging repeated action.
- **Celebration recommendation:** Fogg's own https://tinyhabits.com/rewire/ explicitly recommends positive emotion during or immediately after the behavior; his official toolkit lists small gestures and verbal acknowledgments: https://tinyhabits.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/The-Official-Tiny-Habits-Toolkit-by-BJ-Fogg-Paperback-Edition.pdf. The book identifies this as Fogg's recommendation. It does not adopt the stronger claims that all positive emotion releases dopamine, repetition is unnecessary, or a gesture has been shown to create an automatic habit through dopamine.
- **Immediate enjoyment:** Woolley and Fishbach (2016), author-hosted paper https://kaitlinwoolleycom.files.wordpress.com/2017/08/woolleyfishbachjcr20161.pdf, supports the existing exercise-choice and persistence example. It is not a direct experiment on celebration or dopamine.
- **Human reward learning:** Pessiglione et al. (2006), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2636869/ and https://doi.org/10.1038/nature05051. L-DOPA versus haloperidol affected high-probability gain choices, without an analogous difference in avoiding losses or a significant difference in reported mood. The text uses the actual drug comparison, not an invented placebo contrast. It does not treat a null mood comparison as proof that emotion is never involved.
- **Learning and motivation in rats:** Hamid et al. (2016), https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4696912/ and https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26595651/. Dopamine was measured in nucleus accumbens; optogenetic changes affected willingness to work and reinforcement of preceding choices. The text identifies rats and distinguishes this from humans consciously inferring a complete causal strategy.
- **Instagram:** Harris's 2017 Stanford talk and its indexed transcript identify Mike Krieger as his project partner in Fogg's persuasive-technology class: https://stvp.stanford.edu/av/making-technology-less-manipulative-entire-talk. This is firsthand testimony about educational influence, not evidence that B=MAP caused Instagram's growth. The old transcript PDF URL returned 404 when opened, but its indexed primary-source text was available and the current talk landing page was verified.
- **Clubhouse:** the original 2021 Wave announcement describes an invitation notification and entry into a private room: https://blog.clubhouse.com/wave-clubhouse-new-feature/. The book explicitly identifies the B=MAP mapping as its own analysis. No primary evidence establishing that Clubhouse adopted Fogg's model was located. Features are dated to 2021, not presented as a current app specification.
- **Duolingo:** https://blog.duolingo.com/improving-the-streak/ reports an A/B test separating streak completion from the full daily goal. The reported day-14 retention increase is 3.3% relative, not percentage points. Fewer users met their full daily goals. The text preserves that tradeoff and distinguishes retention from language proficiency. The accessible page did not expose a date or byline; the reference identifies the company and uses n.d. rather than inferring metadata from image filenames or secondary citations.
