| `#rank-control-and-stress` | [Cheng et al. The rice comparison is intentionally framed as evidence for a hypothesis about coordination and transmission. It does not claim that all regional differences replicate, establish a genetic difference, or imply that agriculture explains every East–West contrast. No quantitative figure was redrawn from the PDFs. The stress graph in jy1111 is attributed to Gesquiere and colleagues, rather than to Sapolsky personally.
