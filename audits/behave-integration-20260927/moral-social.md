## Primary-source verification for additions

| Study and inspected source | Design and supported finding | Limit retained in prose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Greene et al.2001, [published article PDF](https://sdimakis.github.io/moral_psychology/readings/week_3/Optional/Greene_2001.pdf), [PubMed metadata](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11557895/) | Two fMRI experiments, nine participants each, 60 personal/impersonal/nonmoral dilemmas. Personal conditions recruited regions associated with emotion; working-memory contrasts also observed. | Associations and preliminary categories do not establish a moral center, independent systems, individual mind-reading or ethical correctness. Main text does not carry unsupported acceptance percentages. |
| Greene et al.2009, [primary abstract and metadata](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19375075/) | Experiments distinguish personal force from contact/proximity and test its interaction with intentional instrumental harm. | Hypothetical acceptability judgments; not actual killing or a normative verdict. |
| Bago and De Neys2019a, [author-hosted full article](https://www.wdeneys.org/data/reprint%20jepg%20moral.pdf), [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30550336/) | Four two-response studies: initial time-pressure/load answer followed by unrestricted reflection. Over70% of final utilitarian responses were already initially utilitarian. | Denominator is cases ending in that response, not70% of people being utilitarian. No claim all judgment is intuitive or deliberation ineffective. |
| Haidt2001, [author-hosted manuscript indexed text](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~jhaidt/articles/alternate_versions/haidt.2001.emotionaldog.pub022-manuscript.pdf) | Theoretical social-intuitionist model and evidence review. | Explicitly labeled a model; not every argument an after-the-fact rationalization. Direct PDF opening failed but indexed author text and bibliographic metadata were available. |
| Ginges et al.2007, [author-hosted published article](https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/satran/wp-content/uploads/sites/330/2015/10/pnas_sacred_bounds.pdf) | Survey experiments with601 Israeli settlers,535 Palestinian refugees,719 Palestinian students, randomized proposal versions. Added incentives increased outrage for sacred-value holders; symbolic concessions reduced it. | Reactions and stated support, not actual conflict resolution; not a characterization of all members of either population. |
| Hamlin et al.2007, [Nature primary article](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature06288) | Six- and ten-month-olds chose among characters after helping/hindering displays. | Preference does not establish a fully formed moral code; read alongside replication. |
| Lucca et al.2025, [investigator institution abstract](https://asu.elsevierpure.com/en/publications/infants-social-evaluation-of-helpers-and-hinderers-a-large-scale-/), [published primary PDF](https://babylab.princeton.edu/sites/g/files/toruqf7736/files/documents/luccayuenwanghamlin_etal_mb2_2025.pdf), [PubMed metadata](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39600132/) | Preregistered37-lab replication;1,018tested,567included; social helper choice49.34%, not different from chance. | Null in this procedure is not proof that infants lack all social understanding. |
| Singer et al.2004, [primary abstract and metadata](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14976305/) | fMRI self-pain versus signal of partner pain, partly shared and partly distinct responses. | Neural overlap is not identical subjective pain, obligatory ACC gateway, or measured altruistic motivation. |
| Klimecki et al.2014, [journal full text](https://academic.oup.com/scan/article/9/6/873/1669505), [published PDF](https://ccare.stanford.edu/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/Differential-pattern-of-functional-brain-plasticity-after-compassion-and-empathy-training-Klimecki-Leiberg-Ricard-and-Singer.pdf) |25women completed empathy-then-compassion sequence;28completed memory control. | Weng et al.2013, [author-institution full paper plus supplement](https://psyc.umd.edu/sites/psyc.umd.edu/files/pubs/weng_shackman_davidson_ps2013.pdf) | Randomized compassion/reappraisal, two weeks.56completers; behavioral analysis excludes15doubting simulated partners;20compassion versus21reappraisal participants. Higher costly redistribution after compassion. | Fifteen post-randomization exclusions explicit in revised prose; selected final sample can compromise randomized comparison. One task, no enduring real-world generosity claim; helping separately measured from fMRI. |
