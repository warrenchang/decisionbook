## Verification of newly used scientific sources

Bibliographic metadata and the particular claims used were checked against the following original studies or authors' original scientific syntheses. Publisher PDFs hosted in repositories remain primary articles. This was claim verification, not a systematic review of each field.

| Source and primary URL | Verified support/design and retained limitation |
|---|---|
| [Paton et al. (2006), Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature04490), doi:10.1038/nature04490 | Monkey neuronal recordings during image conditioning/reversal support both positive and negative amygdala value coding. Does not identify one universal human emotion center. |
| [Cesario et al. (2020), Current Directions in Psychological Science](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0963721420917687), doi:10.1177/0963721420917687 | Original evolutionary-neuroscience synthesis rejects the triune-layer account. Identified as conceptual synthesis, not a new manipulation. |
| [Ochsner et al. (2002), original paper](https://www.psychiatry.wisc.edu/courses/Nitschke/seminar/ochsner_2002_j_cog_neuro.pdf), doi:10.1162/089892902760807212 | Fifteen women, disturbing photographs, attend/reinterpret comparison; affect ratings and fMRI correlates. Restricted sample and imaging association do not prove a single inhibitory command or clinical effectiveness. |
| [Phelps et al. (2004), original paper](https://web.stanford.edu/~knutson/bad/phelps04.pdf), doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2004.08.042 | Colored cues, mild shock, extinction across two days; skin conductance and fMRI. Eleven analyzed learners; same-day decline alone was marginal. Text concerns the overall procedure and brief next-day return, not universal erasure or new neurons. |
| [Eisenegger et al. (2010), original paper](https://uni-freiburg.de/bioklin/wp-content/uploads/sites/337/Eisenegger-et-al.-2010-Nature-Prejudice-and-truth-about-the-effect-of-testosterone-on-human-bargaining-behaviour.pdf), doi:10.1038/nature08711 | Double-blind administration; sixty female proposers within a larger bargaining sample. Offer differences support a treatment contrast. Beliefs about administration were measured, not randomized; motives are not uniquely identified. |
| [De Dreu et al. (2010), original paper](https://rifters.com/real/articles/Science_DeDrew_et_al_Oxytocin.pdf), doi:10.1126/science.1189047; [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20538951/) | Three double-blind studies of men, monetary intergroup tasks, with threat payoffs manipulated in the third. In-group cooperation/defensive interpretation is task-bounded; neither direct racism nor a universal effect across sexes/settings was established. |
| [Berendzen et al. (2023), original paper](https://escholarship.org/content/qt55g324qr/qt55g324qr_noSplash_65e978843a72b7bff801cc572d36ca66.pdf?t=sbcvlk), doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2022.12.011 | Three CRISPR receptor-loss lines of prairie voles retained partner preference after extended cohabitation and parental care. Some offspring measures were impaired. Developmental compensation remains possible; lifelong disruption is not adult blockade and is not a direct human-love test. |
| [Black et al. (2025), author paper](https://landrylab.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/cb.pdf), doi:10.1016/j.cub.2025.07.042; [primary abstract/metadata](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40782804/) | Female receptor-loss voles formed peer preferences more slowly and showed reduced relationship stability and selective social reward. Added the first two endpoints, preserving the peer/mate distinction. This qualifies an inference from the earlier extended-cohabitation endpoint; it is not a human attachment experiment. |
| [O'Brien et al. (2011), author repository](https://repository.brynmawr.edu/psych_pubs/55/), doi:10.1111/j.1532-7795.2011.00738.x | One hundred participants aged 18–20 randomized to hypothetical-money choices alone or watched by two same-age/same-sex peers. No older comparison or brain measure; not actual saving behavior. Full-paper limitations also checked in indexed author-paper copy. |
| [Chein et al. (2011), primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21499511/), doi:10.1111/j.1467-7687.2010.01035.x | Simulated-driving/fMRI study across three age groups with peer observation. Supports the reported adolescent peer contrast; no dopamine measurement and no experimental isolation of the neural mechanism. |
| [Mills et al. (2016), author paper](https://devbrainlab.org/assets/papers/mills_structural_2016.pdf), doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2016.07.044 | Four longitudinal MRI samples (391 participants, 852 scans). Gray/white matter trajectories differ; group anatomical trends are not individual neuron counts or maturity determinations. |
| [Somerville (2016), author paper](https://bpb-us-e1.wpmucdn.com/sites.harvard.edu/dist/b/261/files/2023/03/somerville_neuron16-1.pdf), doi:10.1016/j.neuron.2016.10.059 | Original scientific perspective explaining measure-dependent maturity. It supports rejecting a universal birthday, not another numeric cutoff. |
| [Noble et al. (2015), original article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4414816/), doi:10.1038/nn.3983 | Cross-sectional sample of 1,099 aged 3–20; income associated with cortical surface area conditional on measured covariates. Causal pathway and individual potential remain unidentified. |
| [Nelson et al. (2007), primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18096809/), doi:10.1126/science.1143921 | Randomized foster-care assignment after institutional placement; early cognitive gains. The text does not claim institutionalization itself was randomized or infer effects of ordinary poverty. |
| [Humphreys et al. (2022), author paper](https://sdlab.fas.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum5291/files/sdlab/files/humphreys_2022_pnas.pdf), doi:10.1073/pnas.2119318119 | Ninety-five original randomized participants at age 18 (49 foster/46 care-as-usual); forty additional never-institutionalized controls were not randomized. Assignment effects survived, while later placements could change. No complete recovery or causal parenting-component claim. |
