# Chapter 8: selective deliberation and repressive coping

Date: 2026-09-16

## Editorial decision

Added one example to the existing section on effortful thinking and a related example to the existing section on emotion. No new section, figure, or table. Existing studies and references retained; new entries alphabetized and included in the collected references.

The supplied personality description is not a direct test of System 2 engagement. The revision therefore separates cognitive-demand avoidance from repressive coping. It does not equate either with paranoia, infer continuous deliberation from frontal activation, or claim that orderly living causes stress. Claims about punctuality, meal planning, and emotion-reading ability were not needed for the example and were omitted.
