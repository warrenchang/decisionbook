# Study-centered editorial pass: chapters 15–29

Date: 2026-09-24. Scope: full current canonical chapter bodies, including research lenses, practical applications, and chapter references. Numbers below are filename prefixes; Mental Accounting (20) precedes Intertemporal Decision Making (19) in the rendered book.

This pass distinguishes empirical findings from formal models, illustrations, and editorial applications. A formal probability result or a payoff matrix does not need an experiment to establish its internal logic; empirical predictions about people do. Strong existing study sequences were retained rather than padded with repeated summaries. Existing user edits, topics, stories, references, and explicit anchors were preserved.
