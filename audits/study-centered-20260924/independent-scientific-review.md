# Independent scientific check

A separate read-only teaching fact checker examined the seven priority new descriptions and all four new plot datasets against primary material. No must-fix numeric or design errors were identified. One precision correction was recommended and implemented in Chapter 5: failure to detect an interaction between drug and expectancy effects is now stated as a result of the study, rather than proof that drug effects do not depend on expectancy.

| Claim | Check and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Bastardi & Shafir mortgage decisions | Table 3: 29% versus 56% overall approval, 75% delay; 72% approval among delayers is a different denominator. Book uses the correct overall denominator. |
| Atlas et al. pain experiment | Behavioral factorial drug/control and told-drug/no-drug procedure verified. Nonsignificant interaction wording corrected as above. |
| Hoffrage & Gigerenzer diagnostic reasoning | 48 physicians, 10% versus 46% Bayesian solutions, not percentages of physicians who always succeeded. Auditor access was indexed primary text and authors' institutional account; full original PDF unreliable. Drafting agent also consulted original indexed article. |
| Thomas et al. common knowledge | Paid coordination task, described loudspeaker condition, and attempted coordination supported. Attempts distinguished from coordination success. |
| Van der Bles et al. uncertainty | Five experiments/5,780 participants verified. Small losses in trust remain compatible with 'little trust penalty'; not presented as no loss. |
| Carrera et al. gym planning | Survey comparison, N=877, administrative attendance, and no increase verified. Confidence intervals excluded all but small positive effects; no claim of proof of exactly zero. |
| Rackham negotiation observations | Later primary-report reprint verifies 48 negotiators/102 sessions and relevant planning/questioning percentages. Observational selection limits retained. Independent checker did not inspect complete 1978 article. |
| Four figures | Sana, Oskamp, Small, Milkman published values match `figure-data.json` and chapter captions. Separate experiments, derived SEMs, lack of reconstructed uncertainty, and raw versus adjusted estimates are distinguished. |
