## Verified sources for root-authored additions/reorganizations

- Tay & Diener (2011): author-hosted original, https://louis-tay.squarespace.com/s/needs-and-subjective-well-being-around-the-world.pdf . Abstract and methods distinguish 123-country associations and domains of well-being; new passage deliberately does not claim a causal hierarchy test.
- DellaVigna & Linos (2022): publisher's original abstract/text, https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.3982/ECTA18709 . Verified 8.7/1.4 percentage-point averages, 126 unit trials, and 23 million participants; latter counts omitted from guide to keep it focused. No inference that all nudges share the same effect.
- Open Science Collaboration (2015): existing detailed account relocated into the opening, without new quantitative claims. Separate source/cumulative-evidence treatment and references retained.
- Four new plots: exact reported data, denominators, uncertainty transformation, URLs, and scope recorded in `figure-data.json`; generated by `scripts/build_study_centered_figures.py`.

## Preservation policy

No published study or reference was deliberately removed in this pass. Repetitive advice and generic explanations were shortened, some tool detail moved to optional boxes, and the generic story-update diagram was replaced in the reading path by the donation experiment. Its asset and legacy anchor remain available. Formal models, the approved preface, the Chapter 23 concert scene, CIAO, personal stories, and source/reference qualifications were preserved.

This is a manuscript-wide editorial and evidence-grounding pass, with verification of newly developed claims. It is not a new systematic review or independent replication of every study cited in the book.
