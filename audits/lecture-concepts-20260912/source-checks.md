# Source checks for the added discussion

The lecture notes identified teaching topics. The references below supplied or checked the scientific account. This was a targeted verification of additions and consequential definitions, not a new literature review of every existing citation in the book. Full bibliographic entries are recorded in `new-references.json`, in the relevant chapters, and in the master bibliography.

| Source | Verification and use |
| --- | --- |
| [Menon & Uddin (2010)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2899886/) | Article text; salience-network coordination is presented as a model, not a brain command center. |
| [Spreng et al. (2010)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2914129/) | Article text; default/control network coordination during planning. No inference of a person's beliefs from a scan. |
| [Monsell (2003)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12639695/) | Author abstract and metadata; switching costs and preparation, without a universal multitasking penalty. |
| [Seth & Friston (2016)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5062097/) | Article text; interoceptive inference and allostasis explicitly identified as a theoretical account. |
| [Nisbett, Zukier, & Lemley (1981)](https://deepblue.lib.umich.edu/bitstream/handle/2027.42/24406/0000676.pdf) | Original paper/abstract; dilution defined using information rated diagnostic or nondiagnostic. Correct journal: *Cognitive Psychology*. |
| [Hsee (1996)](https://pages.ucsd.edu/~cmckenzie/Hsee1996OBHDP.pdf) | Original Experiment 1; hypothetical dictionary valuations. The joint/separate interaction is distinguished from the nonsignificant separate-evaluation contrast. |
| [Tversky, Sattath, & Slovic (1988)](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.95.3.371) | Publisher abstract and metadata; choice versus matching and the prominence hypothesis. Full publisher text was inaccessible. |
| [Smith & Vela (2001)](https://people.tamu.edu/~stevesmith/SmithMemory/Smith%26Vela2001.pdf) | Meta-analysis text/abstract; context-dependent retrieval and its boundary conditions. |
| [Tulving & Thomson (1973)](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0020071) | Classic-paper metadata and abstract, cross-checked against Smith & Vela's treatment. The original full text was not retrieved. Used for the encoding-specificity principle, not new numerical claims. |
| [Langer (1975)](https://nuovoeutile.it/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/Langer1975_IllusionofControl.pdf) | Original-paper material and the later primary reassessment below; the historical construct is separated from evidence that choice itself inflates beliefs. |
| [Klusowski, Small, & Simmons (2021)](https://faculty.wharton.upenn.edu/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/36-Klusowski-Small-Simmons-2021.pdf) | Original article; tests the choice-to-control-belief mechanism. The qualification appears both in Chapter 15 and Appendix F. |
| [Mileti & O'Brien (1992)](https://academic.oup.com/socpro/article-abstract/39/1/40/1717731) | Publisher abstract and metadata; disaster-warning normalization is identified as observational evidence. The paper's publication year is 1992. |
| [Read & Loewenstein (1995)](https://www.cmu.edu/dietrich/sds/docs/loewenstein/DiversificationBias.pdf) | Original paper; consumption variety in combined/sequential choices is distinguished from investment diversification. |
| [Skinner (1953)](https://www.bfskinner.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/02/ScienceHumanBehavior.pdf) | Original book, reinforcement and aversive-control passages; positive/negative reinforcement both strengthen responding. |
| [Bowen & Marlatt (2009)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20025372/) | Author abstract and metadata; used to explain urge surfing as changing responses to urges, without claiming that it reliably eliminates craving. |
| [Soto & John (2017)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27055049/) | Author abstract, metadata, and instrument information; Big Five dimensions and BFI-2 labels. No questionnaire items copied; item-attribution correction does not license treating traits as fixed types. |
| [Niiya (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1080/00224545.2015.1095706) | Author's accepted manuscript inspected; direct-request and instructed-help conditions distinguished. Favor requests are not presented as a universal relationship technique. |
| [Collins, Dziak, & Li (2009)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2796056/) | Original methods article; complete factorial designs, main effects, interactions, and design trade-offs. Clinic example is illustrative. |
| [McCambridge, Witton, & Elbourne (2014)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3969247/) | Review results and conclusions; heterogeneous participation effects, rather than a universal productivity response to observation. |
| [Zwane et al. (2011)](https://emiguel.econ.berkeley.edu/assets/miguel_research/21/_Paper__Being_Surveyed_Can_Change_Later_Behavior.pdf) | Original paper; randomized survey exposure, with health-behavior findings and microlending null findings both retained. |
| [Kerr (1998)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15647155/) | Author abstract and metadata; HARKing requires presenting a results-inspired hypothesis as an advance prediction. |

Existing references also support newly visible terminology: Moore & Healy (2008) for the three forms of overconfidence; Levy & Glimcher (2012) for common-currency valuation; Berridge & Robinson (1998) for incentive salience; Wilson & Gilbert (2003) for affective forecasting; Grice (1975) for pragmatic inference; Fisher et al. (2011) for principled negotiation; and Logg et al. (2019) for algorithm appreciation. These were retained rather than replaced by lecture-slide citations.

New examples involving a hiring committee, a reminder study, and a student's phone are explanatory constructions. They are not reported experimental results.
