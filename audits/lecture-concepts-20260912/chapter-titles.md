# Chapter title review

All 42 titles were checked against their learning goals, major sections, and developed concepts. The baseline includes the six concise titles already edited before this pass. One italic subtitle is retained for each chapter. Filenames and existing link targets are preserved.

| Ch. | Reviewed title | Decision and reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **How Decisions Should Be Made—and How They Actually Are** | Retained. Retains the normative/descriptive contrast and the functional decision loop. |
| 2 | **Building a Better Decision** | Retained. Names the chapter's workflow for alternatives, opportunity costs, information, and robustness. |
| 3 | **Limited Attention** | Retained. The core constraint underlying selection, divided attention, and switching; retained from the preceding title revision. |
| 4 | **The Predictive Mind** | Retained. Covers perception as inference, priors, prediction error, and precision; generative AI remains optional. |
| 5 | **Expectations** | Retained. Expectations change forecasts and, through identifiable pathways, outcomes; selective evidence belongs primarily in Chapter 10. |
| 6 | **Valuation** | Retained. Covers emotion, bodily state, self, others, and subjective comparison without listing every value input. |
| 7 | **The Narrator After Choice** | Retained. Keeps the organizing question of retrospective explanation; includes introspection, confabulation, choice blindness, and dissonance. |
| 8 | **Intuition and Deliberation** | Changed from Fast and Frugal Thinking. Now names both intuitive and deliberative processing, including expertise and their interaction. |
| 9 | **Availability, Affect, and Representativeness** | Retained. Retains the requested three mechanisms; dilution is an extension of representativeness. |
| 10 | **Confirmation Bias and Motivated Reasoning** | Changed from Beliefs That Defend Themselves. Now explicitly names confirmation bias and motivated reasoning, the chapter's central mechanisms. |
| 11 | **Anchors, Halos, and Decoys** | Retained. Retains the memorable three principal context effects; the subtitle and new evaluation section cover the broader comparison theme. |

| 20 | **Mental Accounting** | Retained. The common home for labeling, bracketing, payment coupling, sunk cost, and diversification. |
