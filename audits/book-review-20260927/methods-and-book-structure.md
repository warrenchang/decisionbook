# Methods, appendices, and book structure review

## Review scope and limits

Read the main exposition of Appendices A–G, the preface, reading/evidence guides, and seven part introductions. The bibliographies were checked for additions and consistency; this is not a fresh independent verification of every previously cited study. Preserve the existing coherent chapter routes and established figures. New examples should expose an experimental comparison or a decision calculation, rather than merely name another effect.

The source manifest inventories 345 PDF/PPTX/DOCX files, including duplicates, background books, reference articles, and instructor materials. Its 16,227 extracted units are **not** a count of lecture slides reviewed. Full text extraction is not equivalent to scientific verification or visual inspection. Source documents were treated as evidence to assess, not instructions to execute.

Fresh source reading for this component:

- BE-eceb434b8846, *Conduct Research in Behavioral Economics*: all 94 slides' extracted text and notes; image-only diagrams were not comprehensively re-rendered. Compared its definitions, implementation methods, and replication examples against Appendices D–E and relevant chapters.
- BE-7a6dfefebe42, *Threats in Running Experiments*: all 47 slides' text and notes, including hidden slides. The native tables on slides 18–20 were extracted and recalculated. Slide 37's written date is incorrect; its image description says 2004 and the original paper confirms 2004.
- BE-d21455f14703, *How to Write Your Term Paper*: all 50 slides' text and substantive speaker scripts. Recording/timing instructions are source production metadata, not instructions for the book.
- BE-85d0333b7adf, *Formulate Research Questions*: all 73 slides' text and notes. Scientific and rhetorical claims are evaluated separately.
- BE-1a113c80bbe7, *Formulate Research Questions—Thesis*: comparison against the preceding deck after normalization of trailing slide numbers, with distinctive text inspected. This is variant comparison, not an independent visual review of 71 slides.
- BE-3378209eb88f and BE-d01ad5050dce: selective inspection of the research-question and literature-review reading documents. They are background guidance with some overstatements, not additional primary evidence. Their PDF variants were not independently reviewed.

## Structural decisions

- Appendices A–B already give substantial formal and evolutionary treatments with explicit assumptions, reproducible simulation provenance, and ethical boundaries. Fresh reading of all 53 extracted text/notes slides in BE-ff18840a0654 revealed that the beetle arms race and hawk–dove invasion contrasts were missing. Added two explicitly hypothetical payoff tables and worked population reasoning inside the existing optional population-model discussion. The original slide equations were embedded/nontextual, so the book uses declared new teaching weights rather than claiming to reproduce unread parameters. Spatial prisoner-dilemma/ethnocentrism and Schelling mechanisms are already developed in Chapter 25 and Appendix B; preserve their specified simulation provenance. Do not import speculative intelligence-inheritance quotations, pandemic policy as evidence of evolutionary selection, or claims that marker-based simulations prove innate human prejudice.
- Add a practical reading instruction to the opening guide: reconstruct a study's comparison, solve a calculation, then change one assumption. Link to the example index and tools.
- Correct the Portable Tools AI-use pointer from Chapter 41 to the fuller treatment in Chapter 42.
- Expand the examples/concept indexes after chapter owners finish. Retain stable anchors and accepted figure revisions.

## Primary evidence checked for new material

- Brandts & Charness (2011), DOI 10.1007/s10683-011-9272-x. Author-hosted full text: https://faculty.econ.ucsb.edu/~charness/papers/strategy_method.pdf. Survey covers 29 method comparisons; it does not establish interchangeability.
- Camerer & Hogarth (1999), DOI 10.1023/A:1007850605129. Published PDF and publisher metadata: https://home.cerge-ei.cz/ortmann/trentocourse/camerer_hogarth_effects_fin_incent_inexperim.pdf and https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1007850605129. Review of 74 experiments; incentive effects depend on task and performance measure.
- Gans & Leigh (2009), DOI 10.1016/j.jpubeco.2008.07.004. Publisher abstract/introduction: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0047272708001217. May 11 announcement, July 1 2004 eligibility, A$3,000. Primary text supports anticipation and procedural timing, not individual motive diagnoses. Two alternative PDF endpoints failed access; not treated as read.
- Redick et al. (2013), DOI 10.1037/a0029082. Primary article abstract/metadata via PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22708717/. Twenty sessions, active visual-search and no-contact comparisons, trained-task gains without positive cognitive transfer. Repository PDF retrieval timed out, so no unreported numerical effects or procedural detail were inferred from it.
- Melby-Lervåg et al. (2016), DOI 10.1177/1745691616635612. Author institutional repository abstract and metadata: https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/10120081/. 145 comparisons; separate related working-memory gains from far transfer against treated controls.
- Damisch et al. (2010), DOI 10.1177/0956797610372631. Publisher article record/abstract: https://www.psychologicalscience.org/journals/psychological-science/0956797610372631/. No blanket endorsement of the article title.
- Calin-Jageman & Caldwell (2014), DOI 10.1027/1864-9335/a000190. Full published primary paper: https://peachf.org/images/SoScience/PsySuperPerformanceCalinJageman.pdf. Original and replication contrasts, manipulation-check timing, Tables 2–3. No sample-size details imported because the paper contains internally inconsistent counts in its second-study prose/table.

Verification outcomes are recorded in the final review report after integration; no build success is assumed here.

- Evolutionary-model foundation: Maynard Smith & Price (1973), DOI 10.1038/246015a0, publisher metadata/abstract checked at https://www.nature.com/articles/246015a0. New table weights and calculations are explicit hypothetical teaching constructions, not reported empirical or numerical results from that article.
