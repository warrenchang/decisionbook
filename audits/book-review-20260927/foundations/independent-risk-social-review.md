## Finding

No material arithmetic error or unbounded scientific claim identified. One minor wording precision issue was sent to root and the author before EPUB rendering:

- Chapter25, `#public-goods-and-conditional-cooperation`: “Greater confidence that others will contribute raises the temptation's payoff” could be read as a larger marginal incentive to free ride. In this linear game, withholding one extra token raises own payoff by0.6 irrespective of others' contributions. Absolute payoff from free riding rises with the others' contributions, but its advantage does not. Suggested replacement: “Expecting others to contribute does not remove this incentive: keeping an extra token still raises the contributor's own payoff by0.6.” This is a wording correction, not a change to the game's numbers or equilibrium.
