## Other deliberately omitted or routed examples

- Animal supernormal-stimulus examples (turkey/polecat, robin, cuckoo and moths) would repeat Ch06's Tinbergen contrast and Ch08's fly/learned-intuition discussion. The strong moth-navigation narrative in older material was not adopted without checking the newer mechanism literature.
- Suspension-bridge/arousal and facial-feedback/Botox examples were not added as general laws. Existing emotion/interoception plus the new randomized reappraisal comparison teach the mechanism more clearly without importing unsettled causal stories or clinical procedures.
- Universal brain-region functions, triune/caveman shorthand, absence-of-priors claims about autism, direct belief-to-longevity claims, and strong sex-subgroup generalizations were not imported. The book already separates functional frameworks from identified mechanisms.
- Watching-eyes contributions, stereotypes/cultural gender comparisons, calorie-label order, appetitive cue mindfulness, market moods, stock-name confusion, alcohol advertising and interpersonal touch were passed to the relevant other owners where encountered. Passing a lead is not claimed here as verified implementation in those chapters.
