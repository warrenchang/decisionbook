## Scientific and editorial exclusions

The range ledger records omissions locally. Main patterns: untraceable classroom percentages; stale polling/login/payment instructions; unrelated repeated speaker notes; unsupported lottery/health/stock generalizations; old fixed happiness thresholds; mistaken variance labels (standard deviations), tied risk-neutral lotteries, and mixed return windows; high-offer rejection incorrectly attributed to Lamalera; increasing an inequality-aversion parameter misdescribed as greater inequality tolerance; historical anecdotes used as causal proof; speculative neural-localization stories; and redundant variants without additional teaching value. These are deliberate editorial decisions, not evidence that the topics were absent from the source.

Cross-range topics were sent to root: attribution/expectancies, stereotype/math identities, aviation and negotiation culture, COM-B/Fogg, methods and evolution. Country stereotypes, causal language interventions, and stress/health prescriptions were not introduced. Source citations are chapter-local scholarly references, not citations to lecture notes.
