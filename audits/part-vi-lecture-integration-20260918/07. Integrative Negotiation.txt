• Negotiate multiple issues simultaneously
• Make multiple equivalent simultaneous offers (MESOs)

Case revisited: a last-minute demand

After considering these options, the builder decided on an entirely different
approach. He negotiated with the buyer to increase the amount of penalties
if the project was delayed—       and the revised deal made both parties better
off.
The builder realized that the buyer    ’s demand revealed that he was concerned
about delays and he valued timely (and perhaps early) project completion.

higher penalties if the project was delayed, but he wanted a bonus if the
project was complete earlier than scheduled. Both parties agreed to this
clause, and both were happier.
