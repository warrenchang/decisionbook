## Source checks and scientific decisions

- Rackham and Carlisle (1978), original *Journal of European Industrial Training* paper: reasons per argument 1.8/3.0 and defending/attacking 1.9%/6.3%, checked in Tables IV–V. That version describes 49 negotiators/103 sessions; the pre-existing chapter uses Rackham's later 1980 account (48/102). The revised prose explicitly attributes the latter counts to the later report rather than merging the samples. [Original paper](https://altaprorpg.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Rackham-and-Carlisle-1.pdf).
- Jill Carroll discussion: attributed to a retrospective participant account, not treated as a controlled evaluation. [Voss interview](https://berkleycenter.georgetown.edu/interviews/a-discussion-with-christopher-voss-managing-director-insite-security-s-kidnapping-resolution-practice-and-consultant-triad-consulting-group).
- Culture: Brett (2000), Brett and Okumura (1998), and Gunia et al. (2016), with individual, organizational and institutional interpretation kept distinct. [Brett article](https://doi.org/10.1080/002075900399385).
- First offers/roundness: the 2015 lecture reference is updated to the published Backus, Blake, and Tadelis (2019) paper; the analysis is not mislabeled a randomized experiment. [Author paper](https://faculty.haas.berkeley.edu/stadelis/round.pdf).
- Range offers: Ames and Mason's evidence and 5%–25% discussion are tied to their settings, not used as a universal cutoff. [Author paper](https://www.columbia.edu/~da358/publications/Tandem_anchoring.pdf).
- Chandler and Einstein: Shell (2006), chapters 7 and 9, checked in the book text. Shell describes a $10,000 response to Einstein's $3,000 request, with a later package some sources place near $15,000. The chapter retains only the relevant lower-request/higher-offer comparison and labels these reported stories.
- Roosevelt: checked against [Michael Wheeler's HBS interview](https://online.hbs.edu/podcast/mike-wheeler-on-the-jazz-of-negotiation); the account remains explicitly an often-retold case, not archival proof of every detail.
- Camp David: [Carter's September 17, 1978 letter](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1977-80v06/d144) supplies the concrete framework terms; the existing Office of the Historian reference supplies the larger chronology.
- Final-offer procedure: checked against the [American Arbitration Association's explanation](https://www.adr.org/industries/antitrust-compensation-and-pricing-disputes/). Selection scope and decision authority depend on agreed rules. No generic success percentage added.
- MPharm, reservation values and the hypothetical MESO margin comparison: arithmetic is reproduced by `verify-integration.py`; the case assumptions are stated in the text and the algebra is kept in optional mathematical-analysis boxes.

Attachments were used as teaching source material. Instructions spoken to students on slides were adapted into exercises, not executed as instructions to the assistant. No claim is made that missing role packets, film footage, class poll results, or copyrighted slide artwork were reproduced.
