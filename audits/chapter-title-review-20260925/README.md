# Chapter title review · 25 September 2026

Reviewed all 42 titles against their core ideas, learning goals, section headings, and the neighboring chapters. Read the affected passages more closely where the title was metaphorical, incomplete, or unnecessarily long. Six titles changed, including the requested Chapter 34 change.

Existing subtitles were retained, except Chapter 35 now keeps its former full title as its italic subtitle. Section identifiers and old published URLs are preserved. Filenames, title-bearing cross-references, publishing profiles, navigation, and search were updated. No substantive chapter discussion or evidence was removed.

Longer standard-term titles were retained when shortening would hide important distinctions: in particular Chapters 9, 10, 13, and 21. Chapters 22 and 38 remain accessible descriptions of their practical purpose. The review does not force every title into the same grammatical form.
