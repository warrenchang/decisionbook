## Reading and integration method

All 33 assigned topic summaries, boundaries, and original reference lists were reviewed. Selected original PDF discussions were then examined for the examples proposed for inclusion, particularly topics 071, 073, 076, 078, 080, 081, 097, and 099. The supplied HTML correctly warns that its reference leads are not all independently verified. New scientific claims below were checked against primary papers or their publisher/author-hosted versions; the Chinese discussion itself is not used as evidence that a study found a result.

No new subsection headings, mathematical boxes, figures, or personal stories were needed for these additions. The new examples develop existing discussions rather than reproducing the source course's taxonomy. The decisions below refer to the current canonical `.qmd` chapters, not legacy Markdown files or generated HTML. Coordinated additions are completed; their final homes appear below and in the integration report.

## Source corrections preserved in the book

1. **Debiasing:** 19% relative reduction, confirmed by the 2020 corrigendum; source PDF says 19 percentage points.
2. **Surgeon performance:** multiple-hospital observational data, not a randomized change-of-employer experiment.
3. **Reward overoptimization:** synthetic reference score, not directly measured human welfare.
4. **Trophy fish:** historical catch photographs, not full ecosystem biomass; no inference about happiness from smiles.
5. **CoinRun:** behavioral goal misgeneralization, not proof of consciousness or strategic deception.
6. **Multitask incentives:** conditional theory, not a universal claim that performance pay is harmful.
7. **Promotion:** distinguish selecting managers from using promotion to reward and motivate sales workers.
