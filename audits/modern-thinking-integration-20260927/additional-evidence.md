## Specific claims not imported

- The source's interpretation of discretionary-time evidence as proof that a person should operate at a critical point near overload.
- Universal equivalence between thought suppression, happiness pursuit, creative performance, sleep, and social respect.
- Scientific confirmation of a metaphysical narrative principle, a fixed ladder of selves, or the absence of conscious agency.
- A theorem that all life options should be preserved, or that Kelly sizing guarantees gains.
- Claims that AI productivity establishes net job creation or that contemporary model training fixes an immutable human learning limit.

These exclusions preserve the useful questions and examples without importing claims that the cited evidence does not establish.
