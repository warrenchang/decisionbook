# Editorial Revision Audit

## Purpose and method

This audit tests the proposed editorial diagnosis against the manuscript rather than treating every recommendation as an instruction. The review compared the former 48-chapter, ten-part canonical sequence with the revised source manifest, chapter roles, part openers, front matter, evidence guidance, appendices, and recurring cross-references. Word counts use an explicit body-text extraction rule; they should not be mixed with totals that include references, navigation text, captions, or repeated publishing boilerplate.

The revision follows one principle: **preserve substantive coverage by consolidating and relocating material, while removing duplication and improving its order.** Reduction is a means of making the argument visible, not an end in itself. Advanced material remains available when it supports the scientific foundation, but it no longer has to interrupt every reader's path through the core narrative.

### Structural reconsideration on August 29, 2026

An earlier revision treated finance as a one-chapter applied interlude. A subsequent flow audit found that the pause was structurally unnecessary and that the chapter depends directly on strategic resale, bounded strategic reasoning, social learning, and correlated evidence. The final architecture therefore places **Markets, Mispricing, and Bubbles** after social learning inside Part IV, **Strategic and Social Decisions**. Its optional reading-route status is preserved without separating it from its conceptual prerequisites. Part V is correspondingly titled **Persuasion, Communication, and Connection** because ambient social influence has already been developed in Part IV.

## Adopted architecture and continuity logic

The revised architecture contains 41 canonical chapters distributed across seven parts as follows:

| Container | Chapters | Cumulative purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Part I. How a Choice Takes Shape | 7 | Follow a choice from evidence selection through post-outcome learning. |
| Part II. Judgment Under Uncertainty | 8 | Diagnose intuitive judgment, belief protection, context, probability, and calibration. |
| Part III. Risk, Time, Self-Regulation, and a Good Life | 7 | Connect uncertain outcomes, experience, delay, repeated control, money, and welfare. |
| Part IV. Strategic and Social Decisions | 7 | Add strategic interdependence, cooperation, social learning, market feedback, culture, authority, and responsibility. |
| Part V. Persuasion, Communication, and Connection | 5 | Move from warranted model change to grounding and relationship repair. |
| Part VI. Negotiating Joint Decisions | 4 | Prepare, claim, create, and implement value across differing interests. |
| Part VII. Designing Better Loops | 3 | Redesign repeated behavior, choice paths, and organizational learning systems. |

The structure is cumulative rather than modular in the weak sense of unrelated topics. The market chapter remains optional as a reading route, but its canonical location follows the strategic and social-learning mechanisms it applies. Communication precedes negotiation because shared-enough meaning is a prerequisite for diagnosing interests and constructing agreements; decision design concludes the book because it turns the earlier diagnostic mechanisms into accountable interventions.

## Claim discipline for the revised book

The editorial synthesis should be presented with the following boundaries:

- **Pedagogical synthesis, not unverified novelty claim.** The hidden-process and expanded-loop spine is distinctive within this manuscript. Establishing originality relative to the literature would require a separate comparative review.
- **Navigation model, not compulsory chronology.** The arrows support diagnosis and learning; they do not imply that every decision is conscious, linear, or completed stage by stage.
- **Mechanisms, not labels.** Availability, framing, conformity, narrative, and defaults matter because of where and how they alter the loop, not because naming an effect explains an observation.
- **Examples, not automatic evidence.** Running cases illustrate a possible mechanism. Studies, designs, estimates, replications, and boundary tests determine evidential weight.
- **Consolidation, not disappearance.** A removed chapter title does not imply that its substantive material was discarded; retained content was merged, relocated, or layered where it serves the cumulative argument.
