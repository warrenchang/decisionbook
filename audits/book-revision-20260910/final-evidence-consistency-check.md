## Correction reported to root

- `chapters/02-building-a-better-decision-alternatives-opportunity-cost-information-and-robustness.qmd:105` and `chapters/40-choice-architecture-the-environment-gets-a-vote.qmd:82` describe choice overload as more likely when the “decision goal is unclear.” The cited Chernev, Böckenholt, and Goodman (2015) meta-analysis instead identifies a more prominent goal of minimizing effort. Preference uncertainty is a separate moderator. The [primary publisher abstract](https://myscp.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1016/j.jcps.2014.08.002), reopened during this check, explicitly distinguishes these factors. Suggested replacement for the goal clause: “the chooser is trying to minimize effort.” Root was notified; no source change was made by this auditor. Appendix F's broader reference to decision goals is accurate as written.
