Reviewer: chapters_29_41 worker. Reviewed 10 September 2026. Root assigned these additional files for scientific correction after the chapter/figure revision. ## Corrections implemented

- **Appendix A:** The consideration set can include infeasible options, so the later observed-choice maximization now consistently uses its intersection with the feasible set. The paragraph states the nonempty-intersection assumption. The value-of-information cost term now explicitly uses the same cardinal units as the expected value it is subtracted from. The remaining equations were checked for domains, existence, ordinal/cardinal scale, compatible utility components, information bounds, probability/weight thresholds, and intertemporal accounting. No numerical or sign error was found.
- **Appendix E:** The binary-instrument LATE passage now states consistently defined treatment versions and no interference, and specifies monotonicity as `D_i(1) >= D_i(0)` for every unit. This prevents the preceding interference-qualified ITT statement from being carried into the standard Wald/complier interpretation without additional conditions. The original teaching simulation reproduces exactly.
- **Appendix F:** Updated the deadline paper to its **2 September 2026 retraction**, replaced the stale integrity-review description, added the official notice reference, and retained the separation of replication and research-record updates. Narrowed the Macbeth replication to Study 2 and corrected the intervention to copying an unethical/ethical first-person story. Distinguished near-zero/nonsignificant and small/significant ego-depletion estimates and the later protocol actually replicated. Qualified the Many Smiles pen task; specified the outcome and subgroup behind marshmallow attenuation; included money priming's near-zero preregistered subset with its outcome-composition caveat; limited the opportunity-cost result to predominantly hypothetical decisions and removed a conflicting participant denominator. Corrected the Verschuere 2018 author list. Bratslavsky's correct initial E. was already present when the correction patch was applied, so no further edit was needed.
