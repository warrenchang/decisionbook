## Active-generator reproducibility

A guarded helper and transparent exact-patch manifest preserve 23 outputs from five active generators, including earlier reviewed font and wrapping corrections. No embedded artwork or complete SVG snapshots are copied into the patches. All 23 actual generator outputs were rebuilt into a temporary directory and matched the reviewed canonical SVGs byte-for-byte. Cleanup is idempotent and rejects altered, unreviewed generator input. The six existing hand-maintained outputs of build_new_chapter_figures.py remain unchanged by regeneration. Root-owned decision-map, methods and Schelling generators are outside this helper.

Coverage: 10 compact reading figures, two finance figures, two judgment figures, seven part reading maps, and two AI figures. Verification evidence: `/tmp/figure-text-review-20260914/generator-cleanup-qa.json`.
