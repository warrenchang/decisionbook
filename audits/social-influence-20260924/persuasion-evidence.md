## Evidence matrix

| Topic and verified source | Design and result used | Boundary retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Hovland & Weiss (1951), original article](https://fbaum.unc.edu/teaching/articles/HovlandWeiss-POQ-1951.pdf) | Four topics with identical texts attributed to sources of differing credibility; more immediate opinion change under higher credibility, without a significant factual-learning difference. Oppenheimer/Pravda example verified, pp. 637–643. | Main before/after analyses used 61 students present at three sessions; historical student experiment, not a universal effect magnitude. |
| [Kumkale & Albarracín (2004), original meta-analysis record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14717653/) | Delayed persuasion under particular message and discounting-cue conditions. | Does not mean every low-credibility message gains persuasive power merely with time. |
| [Petty, Cacioppo, & Goldman (1981), author-uploaded article](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/229068268_Personal_involvement_as_a_determinant_of_argument-based_persuasion) | 145 undergraduates; exam beginning next year versus ten years later, strong/weak arguments, expert/high-school source. Relevance moderated the relative influence of arguments and source. | Change in attitude toward a student-policy proposal; no permanent classification of audiences or inference of action. |
| [Chaiken (1980), original article](https://dl.icdst.org/pdfs/files3/96885239bf1ed215a797038ba796564f.pdf) | Experiment 1 varied anticipated discussion topic, two/six arguments and communicator liking. Involvement shifted relative influence toward message content. | Argument quantity is not intrinsically argument quality; heuristics can coexist with careful thought. |
| [Petty & Briñol (2012), author-hosted theoretical review](https://richardepetty.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/2012-petty-and-brinol-elm-chapter-in-van-lange-et-al.pdf) | Research framework explaining elaboration, multiple roles of variables, and attitude strength. | Central/peripheral labels are not fixed source/argument categories or a guarantee of rationality. |
| [O'Keefe (1999), meta-analysis publisher record](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/23808985.1999.11678963) | Refutational two-sided nonadvertising messages outperform one-sided messages; nonrefutational messages do not. Advertising results differ. | Corrected the old unsupported emphasis on informed/skeptical audiences: education and initial opinion were not substantial moderators in this synthesis. |
| [Tannenbaum et al. (2015), original meta-analysis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5789790/) | 248 samples, 27,372 participants, combined attitude/intention/behavior effect d=.29; efficacy statements associated with larger effects. | Removed the implication that fear without efficacy usually backfires. No average reversal without efficacy; no universal campaign or long-term guarantee. |
| [Müller et al. (2009), original article abstract and metadata](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19186004/) | Generating anti-smoking reasons delayed the next cigarette; a second experiment examined smoking within 30 minutes. | Immediate behavior, not sustained abstinence. No unverified numerical effect added. |
| [Eskreis-Winkler et al. (2019), original paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6660764/), [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31285348/) | Preregistered random assignment of 1,982 students at seven US schools; approximately eight-minute advice activity; higher subsequent math and target-class grades. | Advice-giving treatment bundles potential mechanisms. Does not isolate self-persuasion, planning, or confidence. Primary full text available via search index; direct PMC open was intermittently blocked. |
| [Feinberg & Willer (2015), author-uploaded original paper](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/282668982_From_Gulf_to_Bridge_When_Do_Moral_Arguments_Facilitate_Political_Influence) | Study 4: 306 US participants assigned military-spending arguments emphasizing fairness or loyalty/authority. Liberal participants reported more support under fairness; conservatives did not differ. | Immediate reported policy attitude. No inference of voting, durable conversion, or identical values within political groups. |
| [Weinstein, Vansteenkiste, & Paulmann (2020), publisher record and abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022103119301842) | Three experiments on controlling/autonomy-supportive speech; final experiment separately varied wording and prosody. Controlling speech elicited perceived pressure and reported defiance. | Listener responses to recorded speech, not observed long-term adoption or proof of workplace intervention effectiveness. |
| [Banas & Rains (2010), author-hosted original meta-analysis](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/John-Banas/publication/233050264_A_Meta-Analysis_of_Research_on_Inoculation_Theory/links/5564bf6a08ae06101abdfc02/A-Meta-Analysis-of-Research-on-Inoculation-Theory.pdf) | Inoculation increased resistance relative to supportive messages and no treatment. | Theory's mechanisms and durability are not uniformly established; chapter avoids general immunity language. |
| [Roozenbeek et al. (2022), institutional repository original article](https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/338515097/sciadv.abo6254.pdf) | Six preregistered randomized studies, N=6,464, manipulation-recognition improvement; 24-hour field outcome on YouTube. | Trust/sharing-intention results less consistent; recognition is not verified factual accuracy or reduced actual sharing; long-term protection unestablished. |
