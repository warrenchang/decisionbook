•Egocentric projection: tendency to use one’s own knowledge, emotions, preferences, and perspective as an anchor when inferring another person’s mental state–Our own mental state is immediately accessible; others’ must be inferred.•The mind uses itself as the default model for other minds and adjust insufficiently•We may assume that others know what we know, feel what we feel, or recognize our intended meaning.–“I intended the comment as a joke, so she must have understood it as a joke. ” –“I would not be offended, so she should not be offended. ”

=== PAGE 20 ===
