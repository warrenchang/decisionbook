# Communication and connection: lecture coverage audit

Source: *DPN07. Communication & Connection.pdf*, 81 PDF pages. Page numbers below are PDF positions, because printed slide numbering is inconsistent. The source PDF was read as reference material and was not modified. Its checksum is recorded in `source-inventory.json`.

The revision retains the structure of Chapter 33 (inference, coordination, verification) and Chapter 34 (understanding, warm honesty, repair). The deadline case and the transition into negotiation remain. The earlier relocation of lie detection is preserved. Every substantive lecture topic has a destination below; navigation and bibliographic pages are accounted for separately.

The main omissions are duplicated navigation, an incidental superpower poll, unsourced numerical chart details, an unverified teacher anecdote presented as fact, repeated variants of question prompts, and course-platform directions. The underlying communication lessons are retained. The teacher-interview example is explicitly fictional, and the class-rating exercise asks learners to produce their own observations. No chart data were invented.

## Evidence decisions

- Newton (1990): the original thesis supports the tapping comparison of roughly 50% predicted identification versus 3/120 actual identifications. [Thesis scan](https://gwern.net/doc/psychology/cognitive-bias/illusion-of-depth/1990-newton.pdf).
- Holt-Lunstad et al. (2010): 148 studies, 308,849 participants, survival odds ratio 1.50. Wording uses *odds*, not a 50% increase in survival probability, and distinguishes association from an intervention effect. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20668659/).
- Holt-Lunstad et al. (2007): relationship ambivalence and cardiovascular responses are supported by the study of 107 adults; wording does not imply random assignment to long-standing friendship quality. DOI corrected to 10.1007/BF02879910. [Primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17600455/).
- Eisenberger et al. (2003): social exclusion and activity in regions also implicated in pain-related distress; no claim that social and physical pain are identical. [Primary record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14551436/).
- Stephens et al. (2010): speaker and listeners were scanned separately; the section describes temporal coupling and comprehension without claiming direct neural transmission or agreement. [Author laboratory paper](https://hassonlab.princeton.edu/document/511).
- Eyal et al. (2018): includes spouse-preference tasks and perspective-getting. Used for the familiarity application rather than reproducing the couples chart with its uncertain attribution to Eyal and Epley (2010). [Author publication record](https://nicholasepley.com/publications).
- Aron et al. (2000): shared novel/arousing activity and immediate relationship-quality change support the added self-expansion discussion; Aron et al. (1997) remains the source for structured reciprocal disclosure. [Original paper](https://files.blogs.baruch.cuny.edu/wp-content/blogs.dir/7309/files/2019/11/Aron_Norman_Aron_McKenna_Heyman_2000.pdf).
- Kardas et al. (2022): verifies conversation forecasts, perceived partner interest, and desired versus typical intimacy. Used instead of citing only the lecture's incomplete “Epley (2026)” chart credit. [Author-hosted primary paper](https://michaelkardas.com/assets/pdfs/Kardas,%20Kumar,%20&%20Epley%20(2022)%20Overly%20shallow%20Miscalibrated%20expectations%20create%20a%20barrier%20to%20deeper%20conversation.pdf).
- Wald et al. (2024): provides published evidence for unexpectedly positive disagreement conversations. [Author-hosted primary paper](https://michaelkardas.com/assets/pdfs/Wald,%20Kardas,%20&%20Epley%20(2024)%20Misplaced%20divides.pdf).
- Bradford and Robin (2021): the supplied slides and an author book excerpt support the three-realities/net framework. [Book excerpt](https://switchthefuture.com/2021/07/15/why-feedback-often-doesnt-work/). The five-move opening is presented as a practical framework, not a separately tested treatment.

The three classroom labs are teaching exercises. Their before/after self-ratings are not described as causal estimates. The source PDF and earlier chapter snapshots are preserved alongside this audit.
