# Image-generation prompts

Date: 2026-09-27. Mode: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`. The final context image was edited using the first illustration as a style reference and the initial context image as the target. Model seed and internal model version were not exposed by the tool. These are original conceptual illustrations, not data or experimental stimuli used in a published study.

## Three windows: generation

Use case: scientific-educational. Create an original, publication-quality illustration for a behavioral economics textbook about framing attention. Wide 3:2 composition, high resolution, crisp flat editorial illustration with simple clean shapes, muted blue-gray buildings, sage-green trees and lawns, pale blue sky, warm brown wooden picture/window frames, restrained navy outlines, white gutters. No words, letters, numbers, arrows, title, watermark, gradients or decorative textures. Layout: upper row has three equally sized close-up views, each enclosed by an identical simple wooden rectangular frame with a thin crossbar. Below is ONE wide establishing view of the same imagined urban street, separated from the upper row by a white gutter. The full scene has gray apartment facades on the left, a narrow attractive tree-lined green pedestrian park in the center, and a broken red fire hydrant spraying blue water onto the pavement at the right edge of the park beside a gray building. The upper-left framed view is the left apartment facade, the upper-middle framed view is the green park, the upper-right framed view is the SAME broken red hydrant with its water spray and puddle. The upper close-ups must faithfully match the corresponding objects and colors in the bottom scene, as three crops of that one scene. In the bottom view, place three thin matching brown rectangular frames over those corresponding three regions, showing which details the upper views select. The bottom frames must be large enough to see but must not obscure the objects. Simple frontal/elevated perspective. Do not put any buildings inside the middle park crop or substitute rain for the hydrant's localized water spray. Scene must communicate three true but incomplete views of one neighborhood, NOT different places, different times, different weather, or a magical window into another world. No people needed. Make the broken hydrant recognizable at small print size. Keep generous white outer margins.

## View and context: initial generation

Use case: scientific-educational. Create an original high-resolution textbook illustration that demonstrates why a narrow framed view cannot establish the character of its surroundings. Wide 3:2 canvas with TWO equally sized landscape panels stacked vertically, clean white gutter and modest white margins. Elegant crisp editorial illustration, muted blue-gray architecture, sage and olive green trees, pale blue sky, warm brown wooden window frames. No words, letters, numbers, labels, arrows, watermarks or captions. TOP PANEL: a broad green landscaped park stretching across the entire width, tree-lined paths and lawns into the distance. A simple wooden rectangular window/picture frame stands in the foreground in the center. Through its opening one sees a short section of a straight pale park path, a pair of green trees, and two small dark benches. The view through the opening is visually vivid while the broad surrounding landscape is slightly pale, as an attention metaphor, not opaque. BOTTOM PANEL: from the identical camera viewpoint and scale, an identical wooden frame in the identical center position shows the SAME short park path, the SAME pair of trees and two benches. But outside that narrow framed view the broader setting is a SMALL green park hemmed in tightly by tall gray apartment buildings on both sides. The framed inner park view must remain consistent across the two panels; the deliberate contrast lies in what is OUTSIDE the frame. Buildings in the bottom panel should clearly dominate outside the central frame, while the top panel's surroundings are an expansive park. This is two possible CONTEXTS for a similar narrow view, not a before-and-after change. No people, vehicles, visual clutter, magical portals or glass reflections. Make frame boundaries clear, give enough outside area to see the contrasting surroundings, and keep the central park opening continuous with its actual surroundings.

## View and context: style revision

Edit image 2 only. Image 1 is a STYLE reference. Preserve the two-panel comparison, frame locations, park path, benches, and buildings in image 2, but redraw the ENTIRE image in the same clean flat editorial illustration style as image 1: simplified vector-like shapes, soft sage-green trees, light blue-gray buildings, warm brown frames, pale sky, restrained shading, no photographic textures or fine grass detail. The top panel is a wide park; the lower panel is a small park among dense buildings. Keep the view INSIDE the wooden frame matched across the two panels: the same pale path, two benches and two green trees, with the surrounding context different outside. Make all visible paths and lawn areas continue naturally across each wooden frame; the frame selects a part of the real scene, it is not a picture pasted over it or a magical portal. Preserve the large white horizontal gutter, add slim white outer margins. No people, words, letters, numbers, captions or arrows. Final high-resolution 3:2 textbook figure.
